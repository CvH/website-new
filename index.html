Why LibreELEC?

Designed for zero distraction.

Traditional Linux distributions carry gigabytes of unused packages. LibreELEC is stripped down to the bare essentials required to run Kodi flawlessly.

Just Enough OS

No window managers, no background desktop bloat, no unused drivers. Read-only system partition ensures stability and protects against corruption.

Seamless Updates

Automatic background updates keep your system safe and aligned with the newest Kodi releases. Upgrade with a single click or automatic schedules.

Hardware Accelerated

Deep integration with V4L2/GBM pipelines, full 4K HDR10, AV1 decoding, and HD audio passthrough (Dolby Atmos, DTS:X) straight out of the box.

HDMI-CEC Out-of-the-Box

Control your entire Kodi interface using your existing TV remote control via HDMI-CEC automatically without setting up infra-red dongles.

LibreELEC Settings Add-on

Configure Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth remotes, static IP address, SSH access, and system updates directly within the TV UI without a terminal.

Rock Solid Stability

Read-only squashfs system structure ensures your TV setup never fails due to unclean power cutoffs or abrupt shutdowns.
